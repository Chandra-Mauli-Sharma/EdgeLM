EdgeLM

Reel Shooting Guide

Seven cinematic reels — you on camera, shot on your phone.

The kit (every day): rear camera at 24fps, portrait/cinematic mode for a soft background, tap-and-hold to lock focus + exposure on your face, one soft key light at 45°, dark simple background, one slow move per clip (no zoom), and screen-record the app for b-roll. Hide every cut with motion or b-roll — never a hard jump.
